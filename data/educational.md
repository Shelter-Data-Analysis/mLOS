# What a survival curve is reading
## The height is a share of the arrivals, not a count of the animals

- Every step down is a day on which an animal left care
  - How far it steps is what share of those still here left that day
- The curve does not wait for everyone to leave
  - A stay still running says the animal lasted this long, and its final LOS is unknwon
- A period is read from all the stays it saw, not just the ones that ended in it

> An average over completed stays throws away everyone still in care,
> and those are the long ones, so it reads short.
