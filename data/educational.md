# What a survival curve is reading
## The height is a share of the arrivals, not a count of the animals

- Every step down is a day on which somebody left
  - How far it steps is what share of those still here left that day
- The curve does not wait for everyone to leave
  - A stay still running says the animal lasted this long, and that is used
- So a period is read from the stays it saw, not from the ones that ended in it

> The point to make from the podium is the second one. An average over
> completed stays throws away everyone still in care, and those are the long
> ones, so it reads short.
